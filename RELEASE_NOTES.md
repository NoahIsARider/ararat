# ARARAT — We Never Arrived

An explorable visual novel about redemption and self-exile on the first earth after the flood.

- Explore the shore, climb the ark and discover three memories.
- Leave in a small boat toward an orange-red sunset and golden water.
- Read rough handwritten English prose with slow dissolves and an ending that returns to the opening through black.

## Downloads

- `ARARAT_The_First_Earth.blend`: editable Blender source with packed textures.
- `ararat.glb`: complete browser scene. Place it in `public/assets/` after cloning.

[Play the exhibition](https://noahisarider.github.io/ararat/). A desktop WebGL 2 browser and mouse are recommended. The initial model download is about 78 MB.
