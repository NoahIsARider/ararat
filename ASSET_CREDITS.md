# Asset credits

All mesh geometry, scene assembly, olive leaves, modelling scripts, procedural fallback textures, shaders and synthesized sound were authored for this project.

The close-view PBR materials use the following photographic scans from Poly Haven, distributed under **CC0**. The supplied 2K diffuse, OpenGL normal and roughness maps are packed into the Blender file and embedded in the GLB. Original JPG maps are included in `public/assets/pbr/`.

- [Rough Wood](https://polyhaven.com/a/rough_wood), Rob Tuytel — ark timber, beams and floorboards.
- [Brown Mud 03](https://polyhaven.com/a/brown_mud_03) — exposed sediment and flood debris.
- [Rock Boulder Cracked](https://polyhaven.com/a/rock_boulder_cracked) — shore stones and keel contact ridge.
- [Poly Haven license](https://polyhaven.com/license).

These material references do not imply that the ark's wood species or the mountain's geological composition is historically verified. No assets from *What Remains of Edith Finch* are used.

Third-party code dependencies retain their upstream licenses in their packages: Three.js (MIT), Vite (MIT) and Playwright (Apache-2.0).

The handwritten narration uses [Caveat Brush](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Caveat+Brush), supplied locally under the SIL Open Font License. Its license is included in `public/fonts/OFL-CaveatBrush.txt`. The manuscript text is original to this project. The ambience and quiet tonal passage during departure are synthesized locally, with no external recordings.
